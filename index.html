For iOS Users

The BondBase iOS app will be available on the App Store very soon! In the meantime, you can use BondBase directly in your Safari browser and add it to your home screen:

  • Open Safari and navigate to this page
  • Tap the share button at the bottom
  • Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  • Name it "BondBase" and tap "Add"
  • You'll now have BondBase as an app icon on your home screen!
